# Contributing

Suggest resources that help agents teach humans AI, build AI literacy, improve prompting, or connect educational workflows to tools.

1. Check for duplicates and choose the most relevant category.
2. Link to the original publisher or maintained project. Explain the audience and one concrete teaching use in a short, factual sentence.
3. Verify the exact URL with `curl -sIL --max-time 30 URL`. Include it only if the final status is HTTP 200. Include the check date and result in your proposed change.
4. Confirm the description matches the resource. Identify paid access or prerequisites where relevant. Avoid affiliate links, unverified claims, rankings and promotional metrics.
5. Keep alphabetical or existing editorial ordering within the category, and update the contents if adding a category.

For broken links, propose a verified replacement from the original publisher or remove the entry. Do not add credentials, private material or personal data. Contributions to this list are provided under CC BY 4.0; linked resources retain their own terms.
